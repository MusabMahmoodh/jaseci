- **Serve: write-conflict re-runs back off with jitter instead of retrying at once**: a request that loses a write conflict now waits a random delay of up to `base * 2^(attempt-1)` (capped at 2 s) before it is re-run, where `base` is how long the losing attempt took, floored by `[serve] conflict_backoff_ms`. Concurrent writers to one node now drain instead of re-colliding in lockstep, so ten clients updating the same record no longer get 409 on a third of their requests. The attempt budget is unchanged; `conflict_backoff_ms` (default 0) is now the minimum backoff base rather than a linear step.
